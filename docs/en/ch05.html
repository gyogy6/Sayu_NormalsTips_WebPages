5. Custom normals

Change only the shading, keep the shape.

Triangles: · Drag the view to turn it.
  • The normals of a smooth sphere (pointing out from the center) are blended into a bumpy sphere. t = 0 is the computed normals, t = 1 is the sphere's normals.
  • As t rises, the toon shadow takes the shape of a sphere's shadow. But the outline stays bumpy: only the shading changes.
  • At t = 1, some places no longer match the shape. How much to blend is decided by eye.
