1. What is a normal?

How the facing of a surface and the light angle decide brightness.

Angle θ between N and L N·L = cos θ Brightness max(0, N·L) Toon (1 = lit, 0 = shadow)
  • N is the surface normal (a unit-length direction perpendicular to the surface). L is the direction the light comes from.
  • Brightness is max(0, N·L) = max(0, cos θ): 1 when the light faces the surface, 0 from the side, and still 0 from behind.
  • Toon shading splits into two colors by whether N·L is above a threshold. Near the threshold, a tiny change of direction flips the color.
  • Move either the surface or the light: brightness depends only on the difference of the two angles.
