3. How to average vertex normals

Uniform, area-weighted and angle-weighted.

Averaging
Triangles: · Drag the view to turn it.
  • A Uniform: every surrounding triangle counts the same. The result depends on how the surface is split into triangles.
  • B Area-weighted: bigger triangles count more. On the beveled box, the large faces stay flat.
  • C Angle-weighted: each triangle counts by its corner angle at the vertex. However a flat area is split, the angles add up to the same total, so the split does not matter.
  • The cylinder caps are split as a fan from one vertex. With A, the shading bends around that vertex; with C it does not.
