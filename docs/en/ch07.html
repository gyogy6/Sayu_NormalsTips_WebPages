7. Why toon shading shows normal errors

The threshold, and how much the surface curves.

Triangles: · Drag the view to turn it.
  • The sphere has bumps so small you can hardly see them. With smooth shading (toon off) they are almost invisible.
  • With toon shading, the shadow edge wobbles. Near the threshold, small normal differences become the shape of the line.
  • Raising “Soften the shadow edge” blends a little around the threshold, so the wobble stands out less.
